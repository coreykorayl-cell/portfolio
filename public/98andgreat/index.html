The ambition

98&Great began as Corey’s creative banner. It is becoming a home for original filmmaking, generous collaboration, and the kind of projects that deserve more room than a résumé can give them.

Right now, the studio is founder-led. The long game is larger: a trusted umbrella where filmmakers, artists, and brands can build distinctive work together without losing the human part of the process.
