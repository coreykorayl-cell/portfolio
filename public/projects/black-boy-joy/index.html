The invitation

LET JOY
TAKE UP
SPACE.

Built as equal parts moving portrait and editorial feature, Black Boy Joy pairs honest video interviews with magazine-style imagery inspired by the visual confidence of Jet.

Why this exists

THE RIGHT
TO BE
HAPPY.

Black Boy Joy was created to show positive images of happy Black men—to reinforce confidence, release some of the emotional baggage we carry, and reaffirm that Black men have the right to be happy.

The project brings together a magazine-inspired photoshoot and filmed responses from three Black men, creating space for honest reflections on joy, identity, confidence, and emotional release.

Building the visual language

FROM
REFERENCE
TO FRAME.

The project began with a clear purpose, then moved through editorial references, location scouting, color, wardrobe, and a working shot plan. The goal was never to copy Jet, but to borrow its confidence and build a contemporary visual world around Black men at ease.
